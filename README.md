### About Me

I like opening up black boxes, understanding how things work, and turning what I learn into things people can actually use.

When I was in primary school, most people around me were playing games; I was more interested in how game cheats worked under the hood. That curiosity led me to programming. In middle school, my interests expanded to software protection, reverse engineering, and systems security. I later discovered penetration testing through news reports and continued learning it on my own.

Today, I work as a Forward Deployed Engineer embedded at a cloud warehouse, where I help bring AI into frontline workflows and apply it to concrete, real-world problems.

### Hands-on Project

#### [`broadlinkmanager-docker`](https://github.com/linw-dev/broadlinkmanager-docker)

While rebuilding my smart-home setup, I started using BroadLink and forked `broadlinkmanager-docker`, adapting it to my own environment and needs.

This project captures how I like to tinker: understand an existing tool, then reshape it to fit a real-world setup.

### In the Works

#### A Native macOS Menu Bar Tool for AI-Powered Text Processing

I want to turn common AI text operations into a natural, uninterrupted system-level workflow:

```text
Select text  →  Capture  →  Process with AI  →  Write back
```

The tool will focus on frequent tasks such as rewriting, translation, summarization, and format conversion, while reducing the disruption of switching between apps. It is currently in the planning stage and will be open-sourced.

- 📦 Deploying practical AI use cases on the ground at a cloud warehouse
